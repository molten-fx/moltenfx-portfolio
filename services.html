FAQ

GOOD TO KNOW

How many revisions are included?

Starter includes 2 rounds and Pro includes 3. Extra rounds are available at a small add-on rate.

What formats do you deliver?

Any resolution or platform spec you need (YouTube, Reels, broadcast, etc.), plus project and source files on request.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to lock the slot, 50% on final delivery. UPI, bank transfer and PayPal are supported.

Can you match a reference style?

Yes — send references with the brief and I will align on pacing, colour and effects before production starts.

What do you need from me?

Raw footage or assets, references, a rough deadline and your budget range. The contact form covers all of it.
